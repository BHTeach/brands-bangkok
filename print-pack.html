Ratchaprasong · Bangkok

CentralWorld

A city-scale stage for retail.

Put your brand among international fashion, food, beauty and entertainment in the centre of Bangkok.

Retail setting
International fashion · Lifestyle · Events
Rail access
BTS Chit Lom / Siam
Commercial snapshot
Two-week pop-up package

Photo: Slyronit · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Credits

The retail character

CentralWorld has a wide choice of clothing and sportswear. Uniqlo, Zara, Adidas and Nike are among the brands here. Visitors can shop, meet friends and spend a full day at the centre.

Around the address

The centre is on Rama I Road in Ratchaprasong. Events and seasonal promotions add to the activity around the shops. There are many different areas within the large complex.

Getting here

CentralWorld is a short walk from BTS Chit Lom or Siam. Raised walkways connect the shopping area to both stations.

A fashion business perspective

A shop here can bring a new collection close to well-known fashion brands. There is room for a strong window display and a clear brand message. Different areas of the centre offer different settings for a launch.

Beyond fashion

Restaurants, beauty stores, technology shops and entertainment bring people to the centre throughout the day. Fashion is part of a much wider shopping offer.

Before you book

Available spaces and nearby shops vary by date. Details of events, visitor numbers and the location of each space are available on enquiry.

Siam · Bangkok

Siam Center

An address for new ideas.

Give your brand a home among fashion, design and new ideas in the Siam shopping district.

Retail setting
Thai design · Street fashion · Creative retail
Rail access
BTS Siam
Commercial snapshot
Two-week pop-up package

Photo: Idea Works Communications · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Credits

The retail character

Siam Center brings together fashion, art, beauty and lifestyle shops. Thai designers are an important part of the offer. Absolute Siam Store sells products from several Thai brands.

Around the address

Siam Paragon is next door, and Siam Discovery is nearby. Visitors can move between several shopping centres in the same area. The BTS connection makes the journey easy.

Getting here

Siam Center connects to BTS Siam at Exit 2. Both the Sukhumvit and Silom train lines stop here.

A fashion business perspective

This is a place for new styles and brands with a clear identity. Clothing, accessories and creative displays are part of the shopping experience. A small collection can sit alongside Thai fashion and international styles.

Beyond fashion

Beauty products, lifestyle shops, food and creative events add to the fashion offer. Visitors can explore more than clothing during their visit.

Before you book

The final space, fitting-room arrangements and nearby brands are confirmed with each proposal. Promotion within the centre is available by agreement.

Asok · Sukhumvit · Bangkok

Terminal 21 Asok

Where journeys meet shopping.

Bring your collection to a shopping centre where local shops meet visitors from across the city.

Retail setting
Local shops · Fashion · Dining
Rail access
BTS Asok + MRT Sukhumvit
Commercial snapshot
Two-week pop-up package

Photo: Francisco Anzola · CC BY 2.0 · Credits

The retail character

Terminal 21 Asok has an airport theme. Different areas are based on cities such as Tokyo, London and San Francisco. Local shops sell fashion alongside restaurants and entertainment.

Around the address

The mall is at Sukhumvit Soi 19 in the Asok business area. Its two train connections make it easy to include shopping in a journey through Bangkok.

Getting here

BTS Asok Exit 1 and MRT Sukhumvit Exit 3 connect to the mall. Visitors can reach it using either train system.

A fashion business perspective

Smaller shops give this centre a different feel from a row of large international stores. A compact space can show a focused clothing range, with a clear display and prices that are easy to see.

Beyond fashion

Food and film are also part of the centre's offer. People can combine a meal or a cinema visit with time in the shops.

Before you book

Space is offered on several floors. Storage, fitting rooms and the shops next to each space depend on the proposal. Visitor information is available on enquiry.

Siam · Bangkok

Siam Paragon

A prominent place to be seen.

Show your brand in a well-known Bangkok shopping centre with international fashion, restaurants and entertainment.

Retail setting
Luxury fashion · International retail · Lifestyle
Rail access
BTS Siam
Commercial snapshot
Two-week pop-up package

Photo: Welcome to Thailand · CC0 1.0 · Credits

The retail character

Siam Paragon is known for luxury fashion. It also has brands such as H&M, Uniqlo, Zara and United Arrows. Visitors can find both luxury labels and everyday clothing in the same centre.

Around the address

The centre is in the Siam shopping district, next to Siam Center. The BTS station and nearby malls make it easy to visit several shopping destinations in one trip.

Getting here

Siam Paragon connects to BTS Siam at Exit 3. Both the Sukhumvit and Silom lines stop at this station.

A fashion business perspective

A temporary shop here puts a collection in a setting with well-known brands and high-quality displays. Spaces on different floors offer different surroundings and nearby shops.

Beyond fashion

Home products, technology, beauty, food and entertainment add to the fashion offer. Visitors have many reasons to spend time at the centre.

Before you book

The floor, display design and signs are agreed with each brand. Promotional support and details of nearby shops are available with the space proposal.

Ploen Chit · Bangkok

Central Embassy

A considered setting for your brand.

Present your brand in a stylish setting with luxury fashion, design, food and hotel services.

Retail setting
Luxury · Design · Dining
Rail access
BTS Phloen Chit
Commercial snapshot
Two-week pop-up package

Photo: Ryan Tang · CC0 1.0 · Credits

The retail character

Central Embassy brings together international and Thai fashion, beauty, design and restaurants. Its curved building and open interior are part of the experience.

Around the address

Park Hyatt Bangkok is above the shopping centre. On Level 6, Open House brings books, food and shared spaces together. People can spend time here as well as shop.

Getting here

Central Embassy connects to BTS Phloen Chit. It stands where Ploen Chit Road meets Wireless Road.

A fashion business perspective

A smaller shop can offer time for customers to explore products and talk with staff. Display units, lighting and packaging can form part of a complete brand experience in this luxury setting.

Beyond fashion

Open House adds books, dining and places to meet. The centre also offers services such as personal shopping and language support.

Before you book

Space design and opening dates are agreed with each brand. Details of visitors and the number of people passing each space are available on enquiry.
