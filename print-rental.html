Retail spaces & rates.

Space for your next collection. Rental options at five Bangkok shopping centres.

Rates vary by location, season, length of stay and space size. Each location has its own rate page.

OptionChoices

Any listed space size can be combined with any listed stay and season.

Setup options

SetupWhat is included
Space onlyEmpty space, ready for your own equipment and displays.
Retail setupClothing rails, a sales counter and a mirror.
Brand displayClothing rails, a sales counter, a mirror, display lights, branded panels and a small fitting area.

Optional extras

ServicePrice
Extra stock storageTHB 300 per day · 7 days: 2,100 · 14 days: 4,200 · 28 days: 8,400
Printed brand signsTHB 6,000 · One-time charge

Your total before opening

Space rental + setup + optional extras + refundable deposit.

Rent covers the full stay shown in the table. Setup is charged once. Extra stock storage is charged for the full stay. Printed brand signs are charged once.

The refundable deposit is paid before opening. It is returned after the space is checked, if there is no damage or unpaid charge.

Staff, stock, payment equipment, electricity and taxes are not included.

An estimate does not reserve a space. Exact dates and the position of the space are confirmed before booking.
