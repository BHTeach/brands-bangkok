Getting around Bangkok

Station links and short walks to five shopping destinations.

Scroll sideways to see the full map.

BTS Skytrain
City trains running above the street. The Sukhumvit and Silom lines meet at Siam.

MRT
Another city train network. Its Blue line connects with BTS at Asok / Sukhumvit. The BTS station is called Asok; the MRT station is called Sukhumvit.

From the station exit

Walking times are approximate, from the station exit to a shopping-centre entrance.

  • CentralWorld

    Chit Lom, Exit 1: about 3–5 min.

  • Siam Center

    Siam, Exit 2: about 1–2 min.

  • Terminal 21 Asok

    Asok, Exit 1: about 1–2 min. Sukhumvit, Exit 3: about 2–3 min.

  • Siam Paragon

    Siam, Exit 3: about 1–2 min.

  • Central Embassy

    Phloen Chit, Exit 5: about 2 min.
