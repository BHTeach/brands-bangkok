The retail character

Central Embassy brings together international and Thai fashion, beauty, design and restaurants. Its curved building and open interior are part of the experience.

Around the address

Park Hyatt Bangkok is above the shopping centre. On Level 6, Open House brings books, food and shared spaces together. People can spend time here as well as shop.

Getting here

Central Embassy connects to BTS Phloen Chit. It stands where Ploen Chit Road meets Wireless Road.

A fashion business perspective

A smaller shop can offer time for customers to explore products and talk with staff. Display units, lighting and packaging can form part of a complete brand experience in this luxury setting.

Beyond fashion

Open House adds books, dining and places to meet. The centre also offers services such as personal shopping and language support.
