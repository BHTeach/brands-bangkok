Planning your presence

A place for your next collection.

A short visit or a longer stay. Our retail packages give overseas brands a starting point in Bangkok.

Two weeks in Bangkok

A pop-up brings your brand to the city for a limited time. It can offer a full shopping experience, a smaller product display or a space to meet customers. Our location pages show 14-day packages in five shopping centres.

Space for your brand

Packages range from 35 m² to 60 m². Depending on the location, a space may include clothing rails, display lighting, a counter or a fitting area. The details are listed with each package.

Setup and support

The setup price covers the items listed on the location page. Extra signs, storage and display units can be discussed. Staff, stock, payment equipment, electricity and taxes are charged separately.

Dates and availability

September dates are available on enquiry. The final offer confirms the exact space and the time allowed for setup and removal. Other dates and longer stays can also be discussed.

Clear rental terms

Prices are shown in Thai baht (THB). Rent covers 14 days, with setup charged separately. A refundable deposit is also required. The deposit is returned after the space is checked, if there is no damage or unpaid charge.

A longer-term home

For brands planning a permanent store, space size, design and lease length are agreed separately. Our five location profiles show the shopping offer and transport at each address.
